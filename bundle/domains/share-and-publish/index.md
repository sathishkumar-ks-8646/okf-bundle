# Share & Publish

* [Share & Publish](overview.md) - API for Share & Publish in Zoho Analytics — covering view sharing, publish configurations, embed URLs, and slideshow management.

# Subdirectories

* [Sharing](sharing/) - APIs for sharing workspaces and views with users, and retrieving share details and permissions.
* [Publish](publish/) - APIs for making views public, managing private URLs, and updating publish configurations.
* [Embed URL](embed-url/) - APIs for retrieving embeddable view URLs.
* [Slideshow Management](slideshow-management/) - APIs for creating, updating, deleting, and listing slideshows and their URLs.
